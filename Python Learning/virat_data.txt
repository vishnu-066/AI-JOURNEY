Virat Kohli is globally recognized as one of the greatest cricketers in history, 
having spent nearly two decades redefining modern batting aesthetics, athletic fitness, and 
competitive intensity. Born on November 5,1988, in Delhi, Kohli showed immense resilience early on,
notably batting through a Ranji Trophy match the morning after his father passed away in 2006 to 
save his team before attending the funeral. 
After leading the Indian Under-19 team to a World Cup victory in 2008, he debuted for the senior 
national side and quickly absorbed the pressure of anchoring India's top order as a generation of 
legends retired. He earned the moniker "Chase Master" due to his unmatched ability to pace innings
under intense scoreboard pressure, maintaining a phenomenal average in successful ODI run chases. 
By the time he surpassed Sachin Tendulkar's legendary record to score his 50th ODI century during 
the 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup, he had firmly cemented his status as arguably the greatest ODI 
batsman of all time. 
Taking over the Test captaincy in late 2014, Kohli transformed the culture of Indian cricket, 
mandating elite physical fitness through the Yo-Yo test and building a lethal, high-pace bowling 
attack that led India to its first-ever Test series victory in Australia and a 42-month streak 
at the top of the ICC Test rankings. Even after enduring a high-profile, three-year century drought
between 2019 and 2022, he staged a grand resurrection, culminating in his iconic unbeaten 82 
against Pakistan at the MCG and a record-breaking 765-run campaign at the 2023 World Cup. 
His international T20 career reached its pinnacle at the 2024 ICC T20 World Cup, where his Player
of the Match performance in the final secured the trophy for India, after which he announced his
retirement from the T20I format to focus on Tests and ODIs. 
Beyond international cricket,Kohli has been the defining icon of the Indian Premier League (IPL) 
with the Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB), setting an untouched single-season record of 973 runs 
in 2016. Off the pitch, his marriage to actress Anushka Sharma, his massive global social media 
following, his lifestyle brand One8, and his philanthropic foundation have made him a global 
cultural phenomenon whose legacy of uncompromising intensity and excellence continues to serve 
as the blueprint for athletes worldwide.
